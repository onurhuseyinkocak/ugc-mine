Profile

Mine Koçak UGC creator in Antalya, Türkiye.

Mine Koçak is a UGC (user-generated content) creator based in Antalya, Türkiye. She makes short phone-filmed videos for brands in five formats: unboxing and first try, Get Ready With Me, demo, talking to camera and venue visits. She writes, films and edits them herself.

Before moving back to Antalya she lived in Bangkok, where part of her paid collaborations with Wilder, ICA Makeup Academy and Kiwamiya were filmed. She works with brands in Türkiye and abroad, in English and Turkish.
