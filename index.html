06 · FAQ

Questions brands ask.

What is UGC?

User-generated content: short videos filmed by a real person on a phone, in the style people already watch on Instagram and TikTok. Brands use them as ads and posts because they feel like a recommendation, not a commercial.

How do I get the product to you?

Brands in Türkiye: domestic cargo to Antalya. International brands: through your Turkish distributor or a retailer in Türkiye if possible. Personal parcels of cosmetics and supplements can be held at Turkish customs, so let's plan shipping before you send.

How long does delivery take?

Concept and script within 48 hours of the brief. Final videos within 5 working days of the product arriving. Rush delivery in 72 hours is +30%.

Do you film venues outside Antalya?

Yes, on request. Travel and accommodation are added to the quote.

Can I run the videos as paid ads?

Yes. Paid use is +30% for 30 days, +50% for 90 days, +100% for 12 months. Running ads from my Instagram account (Meta Partnership Ads) is $100 per video per 30 days.

Will you post it on your own Instagram?

Only if you book a joint post (+$80). I don't guarantee reach. The value is the content you own, not my follower count.

How does payment work?

50% upfront to book the dates, 50% on delivery. I invoice in USD.
