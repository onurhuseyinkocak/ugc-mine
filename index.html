FAQ

Questions brands ask.

Can I hire a Bangkok-based UGC creator for my beauty brand?
Yes. I'm a Bangkok-based female UGC creator specialising in beauty, skincare, fashion and lifestyle content — authentic, aesthetic short-form for ads, reels and TikTok. I work with local Bangkok businesses and with international brands that ship products to Thailand.
Do you create UGC videos for fashion and lifestyle brands?
Yes — try-ons, outfit content, lifestyle storytelling and product integration are core formats, shot to feel natural and brand-safe and delivered ready for organic posts and paid social.
Can you shoot content in cafés, hotels or lifestyle locations in Bangkok?
Yes. Bangkok's cafés, hotels and lifestyle spaces are a real advantage. I shoot on location for hospitality, travel, café and lifestyle campaigns, giving brands fresh, Asia-based visuals.
Can international brands send products to Bangkok for UGC content?
Yes — that's a common way to work. Once the product arrives, I handle concept, shoot and delivery remotely, with usage rights agreed up front.
Do you create content for TikTok, Instagram Reels and paid ads?
Yes. Content is built native for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, and can be delivered as organic posts or paid ad creatives, with raw footage and usage rights available.
Can you create couple lifestyle content?
Yes — couple, travel and lifestyle content is available, great for travel, hospitality and lifestyle brands. For dedicated travel and hospitality campaigns there's also a sister studio, Movia Travel Hub.
Can you create UGC for mobile apps?
Yes. Lifestyle-style app UGC — walkthroughs, problem-solution and integration content — framed in a natural, everyday way that suits beauty, wellness and lifestyle apps.
How do brands collaborate with you?
Send the brand, product, timeline and content needs through the form below, by email, or via Instagram DM. I'll reply with content angles, scope and a custom quote based on deliverables.
Do you offer Canvas UGC for every brand category?
Yes. We create recurring Canvas UGC for brand-owned or dedicated ambassador accounts across beauty, apps, food, travel, hospitality, wellness, fashion, education, services and everyday consumer products. The brief is matched to the right creator and setting.
