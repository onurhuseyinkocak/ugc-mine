FAQ

Questions brands ask.

How do I send products to Bangkok?

Ship to a Bangkok address I share once the brief is agreed. The brand covers shipping and any import duty. Filming starts the day the product arrives.

How long does delivery take?

Concept and script within 48 hours of the brief. Final videos within 5 working days of the product arriving. Rush delivery in 72 hours is +30%.

How many revisions are included?

One round per video, covering edit, captions and music. A new concept or reshoot is quoted separately. Extra revisions are $30.

Do I get the raw footage?

Yes, for +$60 per video. You get every usable clip, so your editors can cut new versions.

Can I run the videos as paid ads?

Yes. Paid usage is +30% for 30 days, +50% for 90 days, +100% for 12 months. Whitelisting or Partnership Ads from my account is $100 per video per 30 days.

Will you post it on your own Instagram?

Only if you book a collab post (+$80). I don't guarantee reach. The value is the content you own, not my follower count.

How does payment work?

50% upfront to book the dates, 50% on delivery. I invoice in USD.
