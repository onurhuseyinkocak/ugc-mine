Antalya · Hotel videos

Hotel video content in Antalya. Filmed like a guest, on a phone.

When a hotel video looks like a commercial, people scroll past. A short vertical video filmed by a real guest on a phone answers the only question that matters: what would it feel like to be there? I live in Antalya and film these videos for hotels, boutique stays and beach clubs, in English and Turkish.

What a hotel video covers

  • Arrival and first impression: lobby, welcome, walking into the room
  • Room tour: the view, the bed, the bathroom, the small details
  • Pool, beach or pier, at sunset
  • Breakfast or dinner: the signature dish and the table
  • Spa, hammam, an activity, or a Get Ready With Me scene inside the hotel

Where I film

Antalya city, Konyaaltı, Lara, Kaleiçi, Belek, Kemer and Side. Outside Antalya, travel and accommodation are added to the quote.

Why two languages

Many Antalya hotel guests come from abroad. One shoot can produce an English and a Turkish version, so the same video reaches both audiences. The second-language version is $50.

Process

A shot list within 48 hours of the brief. Filming happens at the hotel's calmest hour with the best light, without disturbing guests. Vertical (9:16) files with and without captions within 5 working days, one round of changes included.

Questions

Where can I see previous venue videos?

In the work section of my site. The venue example is a paid collaboration for Kiwamiya restaurant at Siam Paragon in Bangkok; the island-day video is a Get Ready With Me filmed at a resort.

Do I need to host you overnight?

Not for Antalya city and nearby areas. If you want both sunset and morning scenes, one night is planned.

Can the hotel post the videos on its own accounts?

Yes. Twelve months of organic use on your own channels is included; paid use is licensed separately.
