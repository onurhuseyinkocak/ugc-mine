Guide · Rates

UGC rates 2026. What drives the price?

A UGC price is not one number: video length, how many versions you need and where and for how long the video will run all change it. Below is my own rate card and the reason behind every line. Other creators charge differently; this is not a market average, it is my actual list.

Five things that set the price

  • Length: a video up to 30 seconds and a 31–60 second video take different filming and editing time
  • Versions: alternative first 3 seconds (openings) are what you test in ads
  • Usage rights: organic use on your own channels is included; paid use is licensed by duration
  • Speed: 72-hour delivery reshuffles the schedule, so it costs extra
  • Exclusivity: not working with a competitor for a period is a commitment, priced separately

What is included

Every video includes captions, one round of changes and 12 months of organic use on your own channels. 50% upfront, 50% on delivery. AI training, voice cloning and passing the videos to third parties are never included.

Questions

Where should I start?

The Single package ($180) if you want to see how I present your product; the Trio if you will test in ads, because three openings show you which one holds attention.

Do you invoice in TRY or USD?

Brands in Türkiye pay in TRY, brands abroad in USD. TRY prices are on the Turkish version of this page.

Can I run the video in ads without a licence?

No. Organic use is included; paid use needs a licence by duration.
